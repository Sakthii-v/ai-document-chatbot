ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & COMPANY POLICY

1. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY
All full-time employees at Acme Corporation are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per full month worked. Employees must submit vacation requests at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal. A maximum of 5 unused leave days can be carried over into the following calendar year.

2. SICK LEAVE & HEALTH POLICY
Employees are allocated 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave is intended for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for immediate family members. For sick leaves exceeding 3 consecutive business days, a doctor's certificate must be submitted to HR.

3. WORKING HOURS & OVERTIME
Standard office working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday (40 hours per week). Core hours during which all team members should be reachable are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Overtime must be pre-approved by your team lead and is compensated at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate.

4. REMOTE WORK & HYBRID POLICY
Acme Corporation supports a flexible hybrid work schedule. Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays) with manager approval. High-speed internet and a quiet workspace are required for remote work days. The company provides a one-time $300 stipend for home office equipment setup.

5. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS & HEALTH INSURANCE
Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance coverage is provided to all full-time staff starting on their first day of employment. Additional benefits include:
- 401(k) retirement match up to 4% of base salary.
- Annual learning & development allowance of $1,000 for courses and conferences.
- Monthly wellness allowance of $50 for gym memberships or fitness activities.
- Paid parental leave: 12 weeks for primary caregivers and 4 weeks for secondary caregivers.

For any questions regarding company policies, please contact hr@acmecorp.com.
